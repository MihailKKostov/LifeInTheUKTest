Q: Which TWO were demands of the Chartists?

- Elections every five years

Q: Which statement is correct?

Q: Why does the UK not have a written constitution?

- Parliament has never agreed on the wording
- The monarch has refused to sign one
- It was lost in the Great Fire of London
E: Unlike America or France, the UK has **never had a revolution** that created a totally new system.

Q: Which TWO are good ways to help the environment?
+ Recycling as much waste as you can
+ Walking or using public transport
- Driving to local shops
- Buying products from far away
E: **Recycle**, **shop locally** and **walk or use public transport** to reduce pollution.

Q: Which statement is correct?
+ Shopping locally helps reduce your carbon footprint
- Shopping locally increases your carbon footprint
E: Local products **don't travel as far**, reducing your **carbon footprint**.
