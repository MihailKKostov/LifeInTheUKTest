# 2
Q: Which countries make up Great Britain?
+ England, Scotland and Wales
- England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland
- England and Scotland only
- England, Wales and Northern Ireland
E: **Great Britain** is England, Scotland and Wales – it does **not** include Northern Ireland.

Q: What is the official name of the country?
+ The United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland
- Great Britain
- The British Isles
- The United Kingdom of England and Ireland
E: The official name is **the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland**.

Q: Northern Ireland is part of Great Britain.
- True
+ False
E: Northern Ireland is part of the **UK**, but **Great Britain** means only England, Scotland and Wales.

Q: Which TWO places are Crown dependencies?
+ The Isle of Man
+ The Channel Islands
- The Falkland Islands
- St Helena
E: The **Channel Islands** and the **Isle of Man** are **Crown dependencies** with their own governments.

Q: Which statement is correct?
+ The Channel Islands are not part of the UK
- The Channel Islands are part of the UK
E: The Channel Islands are closely linked to the UK but are **not part of it**.

Q: Which TWO are British overseas territories?
+ The Falkland Islands
+ St Helena
- The Isle of Man
- Jersey
E: **St Helena** and the **Falkland Islands** are British overseas territories – linked to the UK but not part of it.

Q: Where does the UK Parliament sit?
+ Westminster
- Windsor
- Edinburgh
- Manchester
E: The UK is governed by the Parliament sitting in **Westminster**.

Q: The Republic of Ireland is part of the UK.
- True
+ False
E: The rest of Ireland is an **independent country**.

Q: Which countries have their own devolved parliaments or assemblies?
+ Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland
- England, Scotland and Wales
- Only Scotland
- England and Northern Ireland
E: **Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland** have their own parliaments or assemblies with **devolved powers**.

Q: Which TWO countries are part of the UK?
+ Wales
+ Northern Ireland
- The Republic of Ireland
- The Isle of Man
E: The UK is **England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland**.

Q: Which statement is correct?
+ The Crown dependencies have their own governments
- The Crown dependencies are governed directly by the Scottish Parliament
E: The **Channel Islands** and the **Isle of Man** have **their own governments**.
